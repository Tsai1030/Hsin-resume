我的自傳My Story

關於我Who I Am

從小做事細心、有耐心，面對任何事情都希望完整理解與確實完成。個性慢熱，但熟悉環境後能穩定投入，並用心觀察、學習與成長，常在團隊中擔任整理與統整的角色。Careful and patient since childhood, I aim to fully understand and thoroughly finish every task. Slow to warm up but steady once settled, I often take on organizing and integrating roles within a team.

求學經歷Education

義守大學修習醫療品質管理、管理學、醫務管理實務與文書處理實務。專題〈醫療糾紛與民眾就醫意願〉中學會問卷設計、資料蒐集與 SPSS 分析，培養邏輯思考與問題解決能力。At I-Shou University I studied healthcare quality, management and document processing. My capstone on medical disputes taught me questionnaire design, data collection and SPSS analysis.

克服困難Problem-Solving

專題曾遇組員分歧與統計異常。我冷靜協助重整架構與分工，與指導老師重新檢視問卷、以 SPSS 交叉比對，成功修正並完成研究，學會在困難中保持耐心。Facing team disagreements and abnormal statistics, I helped restructure the work and cross-validated data in SPSS with our advisor — resolving the issue and learning patient, step-by-step problem-solving.

成就感Achievement

在義大醫院負責高科大校區體檢報告建檔，資料量大、時間緊。我規劃每日進度、分批處理並建立檢查表，最終如期完成、錯誤率極低，獲主管稱讚。At E-Da Hospital I digitized exam reports for NKUST under tight deadlines — scheduling daily progress and using checklists to finish on time with a very low error rate, earning praise.
